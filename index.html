Hi, my name is Peter Hua and I'm a software developer based in Vancouver. This is my portfolio/thoughts/digital imprint on the web!

My experience is in backend systems, databases, and distributed systems. Currently I work on an e-commerce SaaS product, where my main tools are JS/Node, AWS, MySQL. I was at one time familiar with these: Ruby, Golang, C, VB.NET.

I'm a big believer in deep learning and understanding what happens under the hood. Having built projects across different domains, I've come to understand that technology problems are very similar and the frameworks and "stacks" are different coats with the same underlying problems. Purely talking about userland apps here; I can only humbly admire people that work on low level systems or specialized software!

I believe in leading with empathy and understanding the user problem (spending time with the user on your software can be a humbling or enlightening experience). The creativity that programming enables is why I love programming! Sometimes the best path forward can be reductive, or a non-tech solution; that's okay because at the end of the day, the technology we build solves a human problem.
